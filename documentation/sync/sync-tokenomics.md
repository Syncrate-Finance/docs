# SYNC Tokenomics

SYNC is Syncrate's protocol token. It is distinct from SGLD, the gold-backed asset, and sySGLD, the asset associated with SGLD staking.

This page describes the current tokenomics model and release schedule. \
<br>

The SYNC token utilities includes:

* **Staking and fee reduction:** staking SYNC to unlock protocol fee reductions.&#x20;
* **DEX bribes Incentives**
* **Governance:** participation in decisions about protocol fees, risk parameters for Syncrate Prime financing activity, and treasury and reserve-buffer policies.

SYNC staking for fee reductions is separate from [staking SGLD for sySGLD](../syncrate-prime-sysgld/sysgld/).

## Unlocks and vesting

TGE unlock percentages in this table are percentages of each allocation, not percentages of total supply.

| Allocation                | Unlocked at TGE | Cliff and subsequent release                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Investors                 | 0%              | 12-month cliff, followed by linear unlocking over 24 months; 36 months total.                                                           |
| Team                      | 0%              | 12-month cliff; 10% of the team allocation unlocks at the cliff, followed by linear unlocking over 36 months; 48 months total.          |
| Advisors                  | 0%              | 12-month cliff, followed by linear unlocking over 24 months.                                                                            |
| Core Contributors         | 25%             | No cliff, followed by linear unlocking over 3 months.                                                                                   |
| Foundation Treasury       | 10%             | Four-year, time-based unlock                                                                                                            |
|  Incentives               | 5%              | No cliff; emitted linearly over 4 years.                                                                                                |
| Community Airdrop         | 100%            | No cliff; airdropped to eligible wallets                                                                                                |
| Liquidity & Market Making | 100%            | No cliff; deployed to DEX pools and CEX market-making agreements at TGE, subject to the applicable market-making loan and return terms. |
