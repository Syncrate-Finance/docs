# SYNC Tokenomics

SYNC is Syncrate's protocol token. It is distinct from SGLD, the gold-backed asset, and sySGLD, the asset associated with SGLD staking.

This page describes the current tokenomics model as of **November 12, 2026**. TGE means the token generation event. Launch figures below are model parameters, rather than a live market price, valuation or statement that trading has begun.

Team and Advisors form a combined 22% allocation, with separate release schedules shown above. No individual allocation split is specified here.

The stated TGE unlocks reconcile to 17,800,000 SYNC: 1,500,000 for Investors, 2,500,000 for Core Contributors, 3,300,000 for Foundation Treasury, 500,000 for Ecosystem & Incentives, and 10,000,000 for Liquidity & Market Making. Team and Advisors have no TGE unlock.

## Utility

The current model includes:

* **Staking and fee reduction:** staking SYNC to access protocol fee reductions. The current tokenomics model does not specify discount tiers or lock durations.
* **SGLD liquidity incentives:** SYNC incentives for liquidity provision.
* **Governance:** participation in decisions about protocol fees, risk parameters for Syncrate Prime financing activity, and treasury and reserve-buffer policies.

SYNC staking for fee reductions is separate from [staking SGLD for sySGLD](sysgld/). The 30- and 90-day SGLD staking terms do not define SYNC staking terms.

## Market purchases and liquidity

The model describes two mechanisms involving open-market SYNC purchases:

* **Liquidity provision:** through the Saffron Finance partnership, liquidity providers acquire SYNC on the open market to provide Uniswap liquidity and earn an upfront premium.
* **Protocol revenue:** a portion of protocol revenue is allocated to market purchases of SYNC.

The model does not specify a fixed revenue percentage, purchase schedule or burn mechanism. These mechanisms do not guarantee token demand, liquidity, price appreciation or returns to holders.
