# Staking SGLD

sySGLD uses fixed-term SGLD staking. Users stake SGLD for a selected term and receive sySGLD representing their staking position.

## Staking parameters

| Parameter                        | Details                                                             |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Asset staked                     | SGLD                                                                |
| Asset received                   | sySGLD                                                              |
| Network shown in the staking app | Base                                                                |
| Terms                            | 30 days or 90 days                                                  |
| Minimum stake                    | 1 SGLD                                                              |
| Yield source                     | Interest from direct leasing of the gold backing staked SGLD        |
| Yield rate                       | Variable; review the applicable rate and disclosures in the app     |
| SGLD-to-sySGLD conversion        | Review the transaction quote and applicable terms before confirming |

The amount of sySGLD received depends on the applicable conversion and accounting rules. Do not assume a 1:1 conversion rate; review the transaction details before confirming.

## How to stake SGLD

1. Open [Stake SGLD](https://app.syncrate.org/stake).
2. Connect a compatible wallet on Base to view your available SGLD balance.
3. Select a 30-day or 90-day term.
4. Enter at least 1 SGLD, within your available balance.
5. Review the amount of sySGLD to be minted, term, disclosed fees and any applicable conditions, then confirm the staking transaction in your wallet.

## Maturity and withdrawals

Staking runs for the selected 30- or 90-day term. Maturity settlement, claims, early withdrawals and fees are governed by the applicable product terms and transaction disclosures. Do not assume sySGLD can be redeemed at any time or that a term automatically renews.

The [SGLD minting and redemption process](../sgld/minting-and-redemption.md) describes exchanging SGLD for supported stablecoins. Its fees and FIFO queue do not establish the withdrawal rules for sySGLD.

## Before staking

Review the terms covering conversion, yield accounting, maturity settlement, early exits, fees and any transfer restrictions, along with the [yield and risk guide](yield-and-risks.md), before participating.
