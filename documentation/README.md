# Introduction to Syncrate

Gold has long served as a store of value, but accessing and using physical gold can be difficult. Buying bullion often involves intermediaries, storage, insurance, transport, and minimum purchase sizes. Once acquired, gold can also be difficult to transfer or use across digital financial applications.

Tokenization can make gold easier to access and use, but only when the digital token is supported by clearly defined reserves and transparent issuance rules. A token that represents gold should make it easier to interact with that asset without obscuring what backs it.

## How SGLD addresses this

**SGLD (Syncrate Gold)** brings gold-backed exposure onchain. Each SGLD token is designed to be backed 1:1 by LBMA-standard physical gold held in secure vaults in the UAE.

By representing gold on Base, SGLD makes gold-backed exposure available in a digital form that can be held and transferred onchain and integrated with compatible blockchain applications. Users do not need to arrange physical storage or insurance themselves to hold SGLD.

Syncrate’s approach combines physical reserves with onchain issuance rules:

* **Physical gold backing:** SGLD is backed by LBMA-standard gold held in UAE vaults.
* **Independent verification:** The physical reserves are subject to independent audits conducted by Bureau Veritas.
* **Onchain supply:** SGLD’s token supply and smart-contract activity can be inspected onchain.
* **Controlled issuance:** SGLD is minted through the protocol’s deposit process, which is designed to maintain the relationship between issued tokens and their backing.
* **Pricing infrastructure:** Chainlink provides pricing infrastructure used by the protocol.

These measures are intended to make the relationship between SGLD and its underlying gold easier to understand and verify. Reserve verification and smart-contract rules serve different purposes: audits provide evidence about physical holdings, while onchain data makes token supply and contract activity visible.

## From gold ownership to productive gold

SGLD is the foundation of Syncrate’s broader work to bring real-world assets into onchain finance. It provides gold-backed exposure; it does not itself generate yield.

Syncrate Prime is a separate product designed to give eligible SGLD holders access to yield generated through short-term gold-financing activity with bullion banks and refiners. This separates holding gold-backed exposure through SGLD from choosing to participate in a financing strategy through Prime.

## The principle behind Syncrate

Syncrate is built around a simple idea: real-world assets should be easier to access and use onchain, while the assets behind them and the rules governing their tokens remain clear.
